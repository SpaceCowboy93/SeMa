# SeMa Philosophy

---

SeMa is not just a planner.

It is not a task manager.

It is not a calendar.

It is not a productivity tool.

SeMa is a beautiful little world shared by two people.

Every feature should strengthen that feeling.

If a feature makes the app busier instead of warmer, simpler or more meaningful, it should be reconsidered.

---

## Our Goal

We don't want users to open SeMa because they have to.

We want them to open it because they enjoy opening it.

The app should feel like coming home.

---

## Our Design Values

**1. Calm.**

No loud UI.
No unnecessary notifications.
No visual overload.
No feature should shout.

**2. Warm.**

The interface should feel welcoming.
Personal greetings.
Small emotional touches.
Natural language.

**3. Alive.**

The app quietly changes as life changes.

Morning. Afternoon. Evening.
Birthdays. Anniversaries. Trips. Christmas.
Goals achieved. Vacation. Meaningful moments.

Never random. Never overwhelming.

**4. Personal.**

Mateo should feel the app belongs to him.
Seval should feel the app belongs to her.
The personality adapts. The functionality stays identical.

**5. Beautiful.**

Whitespace. Typography. Animation. Motion.
Every detail should feel intentional.

---

## Living Moments

Instead of themes, SeMa uses Living Moments.

These are subtle. They never interrupt the user. They simply make the app feel alive.

| Moment | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| ❤️ Anniversary tomorrow | Tiny flowers. Greeting changes. |
| 🎂 Birthday | Small celebration. Warm greeting. Confetti once. |
| ✈️ Trip tomorrow | Tiny airplane. Adventure greeting. |
| 🏖 Vacation starts | Warm sunny feeling. |
| 💰 Savings goal reached | Small sparkle. |
| 💌 New love note | Envelope beside greeting. |
| 🎄 Christmas | Warm lights. Seasonal greeting. |

---

## Relationship Profile

In the future every user will have a complete profile.

Examples:

- Birthday
- Favourite flowers / colour / perfume
- Favourite food / restaurants / coffee
- Favourite movies / music
- Dream destinations
- Gift wishlist
- Clothing size / ring size / shoe size
- Important dates
- Dream goals

This information powers future AI features and Living Moments.
Nothing is hardcoded. Everything is user configurable.

---

## AI Philosophy

AI is not the product.

AI is the companion. It should help. Never replace human connection.

Examples: gift ideas, trip planning, date planning, budget planning, conversation starters, relationship insights, memory suggestions, weekly summaries.

---

## The Feeling

When someone opens SeMa after six months, they should not think:

> *"This is a useful app."*

They should think:

> *"I love opening this."*

---

## Final Principle

Every design decision should answer one question:

**"Does this help two people enjoy life together?"**

If the answer is no — do not build it.
